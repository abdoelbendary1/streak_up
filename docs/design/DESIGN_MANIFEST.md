# StreakUp Design Manifest

This is the authoritative registry for StreakUp design assets. Only **APPROVED** images may be used as implementation references. The Product Owner explicitly approved the final images supplied for consolidation, the five SL-01 variants, and the nine SL-02 images registered below. Their repository copies retain the original bytes. SL02-UI-008 is RETIRED by the 2026-09-30 Product Owner decision; eight SL-02 states remain active and approved. RETIRED images are historical only and must not guide new v1 implementation. Approval applies to the stated image and scope, not to unshown variants or product behavior. Update this manifest whenever a new design is approved.

## Status values

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| PENDING | Asset or approval is not yet available. |
| IN_REVIEW | Submitted for approval; not authoritative. |
| APPROVED | Explicitly approved for its stated role and scope. |
| SUPERSEDED | Replaced by a later approved entry; no longer authoritative. |

## Additional files and missing variants

The existing [Today feature copy](screens/001-bilingual-shell-feedback/today-en.png) and [earlier Today reference filename](references/REF-001-streakup-master.png) are byte-identical to `REF-001-today.png` (SHA-256 begins `9450CE322BC17DFC`). They are aliases of `REF-001`, not separate design decisions. Keep their existing filenames for references already using them; use the canonical `REF-001` path for new citations.

The approved SL-01 images cover the previously missing Arabic RTL shell, a Review entry to Sync & Recovery, large text, offline, and sync-failure presentation. Reconnecting and Syncing remain semantic states defined in the approved design system and SL-01 specification; they do not require separate PNGs for technical planning. Other feature screens and state variants are not approved by implication. When an approved image is replaced, mark its former entry SUPERSEDED and identify the replacement.
