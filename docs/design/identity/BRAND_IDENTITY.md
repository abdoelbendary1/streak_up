## Brand character and mark

**Name:** StreakUp.

StreakUp is optimistic, encouraging, calm, and practical. Its visual tone celebrates small daily steps and steady growth without shaming missed days or making social competition dominate personal habit work. The board's line, “Small steps today for a brighter tomorrow,” captures that tone.

The logo concept combines a **flame** for streak energy with a **leaf/growth** form for personal progress. The approved `LOGO-001` file is the standalone horizontal wordmark: green symbol, navy “Streak,” and green “Up.” Use that asset for primary brand identification on a surface where it remains legible. The approved `ICON-001` file is the standalone green flame/leaf symbol on a light field for application-icon use. Use the supplied artwork without recreating it from the identity board. Platform export sizes, masking, and contrast need implementation validation; no minimum size or clear-space measurements are approved.

## Typography and language

Typography is clean, friendly, and highly readable. Strong headings carry the optimistic message; quieter supporting text keeps the daily UI calm. The board illustrates heading, body, and caption hierarchy in English and Arabic. No exact font family is approved; font choice and rendered metrics need implementation validation. English LTR and Arabic RTL are first-class directions. Layout, alignment, icon direction, copy length, and line height must work in both languages and with large text. An approved Arabic screen reference is still required by the SL-01 design gate.

## Visual language

- **Iconography:** Simple, recognizable activity and utility icons with clear silhouettes. Green can mark exercise and growth; blue, purple, peach, and orange provide differentiated activity accents. Icons need text or accessible names when they convey state or action.
- **Rounded shapes:** Softly rounded cards and buttons, circular icon containers, and pill-like selected states create an approachable feel. No exact radius is established here.
- **Cards and surfaces:** White cards on a pale background use gentle boundaries and subtle depth. Positive callouts may use a soft green tint. Keep content easy to scan.
- **Illustration and icon containers:** Soft tinted circles frame activity symbols and small growth illustrations. They add warmth and context without replacing labels.
- **Personal streak:** Use the flame, a labeled day count, and continuity/progress cues. The streak is a personal consistency measure.
- **Challenge:** Use trophy and goal/progress cues for a separate competitive context. Challenge score is distinct from personal streak count.
- **Private / Shared:** Use the words “Private” and “Shared” with lock or people icons. Do not depend on tint alone to communicate visibility.
- **Offline / Sync:** Use a status icon with explicit text for Synced, Pending sync, Offline, and other connection states. Pending uses orange; information and failures use their semantic roles. A completed local action can still be Pending remotely.
